07 / HUMAN CONTROL

AI handles the routine.
People handle the important.

The objective is not to remove humans from the customer journey. It is to make human attention more valuable.

RULE 01

Escalate uncertainty

If confidence is low, the request falls outside approved knowledge, or a policy requires review, route to a human.

RULE 02

Preserve context

The employee receives a concise summary, customer details, intent, collected data and the reason for escalation.

RULE 03

Keep an audit trail

Important AI actions can be logged so teams can understand what happened and improve the workflow.

RULE 04

Measure the gaps

Handoffs and failed answers become signals for improving knowledge, rules and customer journeys.
